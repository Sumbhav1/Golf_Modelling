# Forward test: season 2026 (in progress, updated as events finish)

Generated by `uv run python -m src.models.run_forward_test` from `config/config.yaml`.
**This is not a frozen evaluation.** It is re-run as more of the 2026 season finishes;
today it covers 37 completed events. Settings are re-derived (never re-tuned) from the
2023-2024 validation seasons, identically to `reports/holdout_2025_results.md`: Baseline 1's
rolling-strokes-gained window per label (Make the cut: 3-season, Top 10: 3-season, Win: 3-season); Model 1's rating half-life
365 days and shrinkage 30 pseudo-rounds. Both models are refit on seasons
[2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025] (everything through the frozen 2025 holdout, now historical training data) and
scored on the 2026 events finished so far. Differences are model minus reference, so
**negative means better**. Intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals over events (1000 resamples,
seed 42); differences against Baseline 1 use the same resampled events for both, so they are
paired. Sample size is small and grows through the season - read the intervals, not just the point
estimates.
